Privacy Policy

For the game If I Fits, I Sits

Last updated: 12 September 2026

Short version: the browser version collects nothing and has no internet access. The Android app records anonymous gameplay statistics, so I can see which levels people get stuck on, and shows adverts. Neither knows who you are. Your progress stays on your own device.

What this covers

This policy applies to the game If I Fits, I Sits by Faisal Lumintang, in both forms it ships in: the Android app on Google Play (package com.burangkeng.fitsandsits) and the browser version hosted on this site. They share the same game code, but only the Android app collects anything: the browser version has no adverts and no analytics.

A copy of the browser version is also published on itch.io. That copy is additionally subject to itch.io's own privacy policy, since they host it.

What the game collects

No personal data. The Android app records anonymous gameplay statistics, described under Analytics below, and shows adverts, described under Advertising and purchases. Beyond those two, nothing is collected. In particular the game never collects:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers or any contact details
  • Accounts or logins — the game has none
  • Location data of any kind, beyond which country you are in
  • Photos, files, contacts, microphone or camera access
  • Anything you type

Analytics

The Android app only. It uses GameAnalytics to record how the game is played. This exists for one reason: to show which levels players get stuck on, so they can be fixed. What it records:

  • a randomly generated identifier for this installation, created on the device — it is not your advertising ID, and it is not linked to any account
  • device model, operating system version and country
  • gameplay events: a level being started, finished or given up on, how many moves it took, hints being used, and purchases completing

None of it identifies you, none of it is sold, and none of it is used to target advertising. Uninstalling the app removes the identifier along with it. The browser version sends none of this.

Network access

The browser version is entirely self-contained: it opens no connections and calls no server. The Android app reaches the network for three things and nothing else — adverts, the analytics described above, and Google Play Billing when you buy something. Gameplay itself never needs a connection, and the game is fully playable offline.

What is stored on your device

Your progress — which levels you have completed, your best move count on each, and your hint balance — is written to a single save file in the game's private storage. It never leaves your device, and no other app can read it. In the browser version the equivalent data lives in that browser's local storage for this site, and likewise never leaves your machine.

To delete it: uninstall the Android app, and Android removes its private storage along with it. For the browser version, clear site data for this domain. Nothing is retained anywhere else, because there is nowhere else.

Children's privacy

The game is suitable for all ages. It asks for no personal information from anyone, including children under 13, and the analytics identifier is random rather than tied to a person. To request deletion of the data associated with an installation, email the address below; because the identifier is not linked to an account, please send it from the device or include it when you write.

What Google Play collects

The game itself sends nothing, but Google Play — the store you installed the Android version from — collects its own data such as install counts, device model and country, plus any crash reports the Android system reports automatically. That is handled by Google, not by this game, and is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. The developer sees only anonymous, aggregated statistics in the Play Console.

Advertising and purchases

The browser version of the game shows no advertising and has nothing to buy.

The Android app is ad-supported. Advertising is served by Google AdMob. Adverts are never forced: the only one is a rewarded video you choose to watch in exchange for hints. The advertising SDK communicates with Google's servers and collects:

  • your device's advertising identifier, which is resettable and can be cleared in Android's settings
  • coarse device and app information, such as model, operating system version and country, used to choose which advert to show
  • whether an advert was shown, viewed or clicked

That data goes to Google, not to the developer, and is governed by the Google Advertising Policy. The developer sees only aggregate revenue figures. You can limit ad personalisation at any time under Settings › Privacy › Ads on your device, and you can simply not watch the video, which is the only advert the game shows.

There are two things to buy, both of them hints: a pack of 25, and unlimited hints. Neither removes advertising, because there is no advertising to remove beyond the video you opt into. Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing, and the developer never sees your payment details.

Everything else in this policy still holds: nothing collected identifies you, and your progress never leaves your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date. Material changes affecting what is collected will be reflected in the Play listing before the relevant update ships.
