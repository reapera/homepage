Privacy Policy

For the game If I Fits, I Sits

Last updated: 4 September 2026

Short version: the game collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no internet access. Your progress is a single file on your own device.

What this covers

This policy applies to the game If I Fits, I Sits by Faisal Lumintang, in both forms it ships in: the Android app on Google Play (package com.burangkeng.fitsandsits) and the browser version hosted on this site. The same build logic underlies both, and neither collects anything.

A copy of the browser version is also published on itch.io. That copy is additionally subject to itch.io's own privacy policy, since they host it.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Specifically, it does not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers or any contact details
  • Accounts or logins — the game has none
  • Location data of any kind
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs or usage analytics
  • Photos, files, contacts, microphone or camera access
  • Crash or diagnostic reports

Network access

The Android app requests no permissions beyond the platform defaults, and neither version contains any networking code. The game does not open connections, call any server, or include analytics, advertising or third-party SDKs. The Android version works identically with the device offline, because it is always effectively offline.

What is stored on your device

Your progress — which levels you have completed, your best move count on each, and your hint balance — is written to a single save file in the game's private storage. It never leaves your device, and no other app can read it. In the browser version the equivalent data lives in that browser's local storage for this site, and likewise never leaves your machine.

To delete it: uninstall the Android app, and Android removes its private storage along with it. For the browser version, clear site data for this domain. Nothing is retained anywhere else, because there is nowhere else.

Children's privacy

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13. Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to request the deletion of, and no parental consent mechanism is required under COPPA or the GDPR.

What Google Play collects

The game itself sends nothing, but Google Play — the store you installed the Android version from — collects its own data such as install counts, device model and country, plus any crash reports the Android system reports automatically. That is handled by Google, not by this game, and is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. The developer sees only anonymous, aggregated statistics in the Play Console.

Advertising and purchases

The current version contains no advertising and no in-app purchases. Hints are earned through play. If a future version adds either, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the Play listing's Data safety section will be updated to match.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date. Material changes affecting what is collected will be reflected in the Play listing before the relevant update ships.
